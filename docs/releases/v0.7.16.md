# Melodex v0.7.16

## Campaign V feedback closure

- Album Wall uses calmer label visibility and wider spacing, reuses prepared covers during rapid panning and refreshes, and plays albums in disc and track order.
- Profile Pulse draws more concentric contours across the scene.
- Sonic Weather and Minimal are removed from the visualizer choices.
- Music Map shows album covers, expands track details on hover, and provides zoom controls with smaller points for dense overviews.
- Now Playing photo attribution appears on hover rather than covering the photo.

Changed visual scenes and browsing screens are captured at 1024×768, 1280×800, and 1440×900. The release is gated on regression, visual, and platform packaging checks.
