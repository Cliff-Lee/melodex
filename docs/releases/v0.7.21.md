# Melodex v0.7.21 — Android Standalone Player Preview

This release starts P19 with P19a: Android can play music stored on the phone
without requiring a computer. Provider Bridge remains available as an optional
way to connect to sources configured on another Melodex installation.

## On this phone

- Request audio-library permission only after the listener chooses **Allow
  music access**.
- List audio items indexed by Android across attached external volumes.
- Play a selected item directly from its content URI, or choose **Play
  something** for a random track.
- Keep phone audio on the device; Melodex does not copy it to the Bridge or
  scan filesystem paths.
- Refresh the list after adding music.

## Optional Provider Bridge

The Bridge path remains available for searching and playing sources configured
on a desktop, NAS, or home server. The Android app does not run downloaded
provider code. Bridge address and token are entered manually; QR pairing and
automatic discovery are not included.

## Preview limits and testing

This is the first P19 slice. Playback is owned by the foreground activity, so
background playback, notification/lock-screen and headset controls, a
persistent queue, local search, artwork, and Flow/taste controls remain future
work.

The Android CI build passed. Please check permission grant/denial, an empty and
populated phone library, and direct playback on Android 12-or-older and Android
13-or-newer devices. Report device/version and any failing step with feedback.

## Packages

The release workflow attaches Android APK/AAB, macOS and Windows packages,
Linux packages, and the source archive after platform build and smoke checks
complete.
